Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR & Global Standards

At RC Techno Solution, we treat your privacy and proprietary project data with the highest level of security and confidentiality.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver software engineering and consulting services:

  • Contact Information: Name, professional email address, phone number, and company name when you fill out our contact or consultation forms.
  • Project Specifications: Technical requirements, project briefs, and desired tech stacks submitted through our discovery tools.
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized browser type, operating system, and page performance telemetrics to ensure optimal web experience.

2. How We Use and Protect Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to:

  • Provide accurate project proposals, architecture assessments, and consultations.
  • Maintain ongoing communication during software engineering sprints.
  • Deliver customer support, maintenance SLA updates, and technical notifications.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers or brokers.

3. Security Standards

We enforce rigorous security controls including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption in transit, strict access control lists (ACLs), multi-factor authentication (MFA) across our development tools, and encrypted credential storage.
